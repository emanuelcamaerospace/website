IST · Portuguese Air Force · ISR Sep 2025 – Nov 2026
MSc thesis

Laser-based interception of unmanned aerial systems

A low-power counter-drone system that blinds a drone's camera instead of destroying it. The hard part moves from laser power to tracking and control: keep a modest laser locked on the sensor long enough to neutralise it.

  • Integral Sliding Mode Controller, tuned with a genetic algorithm, on a precision 2-axis gimbal
  • End-to-end Simulink framework: gimbal dynamics, vision, noise, target physics
  • 3D-printed prototype with Pixhawk avionics, validated in field tests

About

It has always been my dream to fly. That first pointed me towards becoming an airline pilot; today I channel it into flight simulation and into building the machines themselves.

That fascination pushed me into Portugal's most competitive engineering programme, Aerospace Engineering at Instituto Superior Técnico. I graduated from high school with 19.13/20 and moved from a rural village to Lisbon to study.

My first real technical impact came at ANAC, building software that predicts where a drone lands after a critical failure. Since then I've studied propulsion at TU Munich, joined the BLUE turbojet team, and taken on a counter-drone thesis with the Portuguese Air Force.
